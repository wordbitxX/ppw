## 2. PHASE 2 — Review engine (ongoing, the real moat)

Nobody ranks or converts in B2B without reviews; your competitors all have them.

1. **Review links (save these):**
   - Google: from GBP → "Ask for reviews" (auto-generates a direct review link for your business).
   - Clutch: Clutch's own client-review flow (client must be a verified project client — that's what makes it worth something).
2. **The 3-message script (WhatsApp/email) — send to every past or current client, including small PK jobs:**
   - M1 (project handover): *"Project delivered — thank you. If working with the team was smooth, a 2-minute Google review genuinely helps a small Lahore company more than anything else: [link]. And if you ever need phase 2, here's a 10% priority slot."*
   - M2 (2 weeks later, no review): gentle nudge + offer to call.
   - M3 (monthly, only if they said yes to Clutch later): Clutch review ask.
3. **Rate expectation:** with ~20 past touches you should bank **5-8 Google reviews in 60 days**. Publish the count on the homepage only when ≥5 (visible change — needs your OK, see audit Q5).
4. **Never buy, never incentivize with money.** A discount for a *service* is fine; payment for a review is a policy violation on Google, Clutch and AdSense.

## 4. What NOT to do (protects the site)

- ❌ Buy links, PBNs, "SEO packages" with guaranteed backlinks — your site is young and clean; a spam signal now = months of recovery.
- ❌ Fake reviews / incentivized reviews — Google, Clutch and AdSense all police this (see audit §2.1 for why).
- ❌ Different NAP across directories — one address, one phone, one spelling, everywhere.
- ❌ Keyword-stuffed GBP name/categories (suspension risk).
- ❌ Adding 100 thin pages to "increase indexation" — you already have 224 quality URLs; depth + authority beats volume (the 16 hire-developer pages are the only approved exception, and they'll be full content).

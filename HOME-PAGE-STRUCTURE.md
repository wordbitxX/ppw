# Home Page Structure — diagnosis and recommended order

**Date:** 3 October 2026
**Question asked:** the page feels mis-managed. Which sections are necessary, and what order should they be in?
**Status: implemented.** Steps 1–6 in §5 are all shipped. Measured results are in §4.

---

## 1. Why it feels wrong — the actual diagnosis

Every section on this page belongs to one of six themes. Here is the page in its current order, with each section tagged:

| # | Section (h2) | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | **HOOK** |
| 2 | Assurance strip (4 promises) | **WHY US** |
| 3 | A software team that thinks about your business (4 photos + 4 pillars) | **WHY US** |
| 4 | Software, stores, SEO and marketing (8 service cards) | **WHAT** |
| 5 | See our work live — demos | **PROOF** |
| 6 | Ready-to-adapt platforms for the industries we know best | **WHO FOR** |
| 7 | Three areas where we deliver the most value | **WHAT** |
| 8 | WordbitX is trusted to deliver work you can inspect (orbit) | **WHAT + PROOF** |
| 9 | Reasons clients keep working with us | **WHY US** |
| 10 | A stack chosen for the problem, not for fashion | **HOW** |
| 11 | Software delivery process from discovery to launch | **HOW** |
| 12 | Software for retail, real estate, healthcare and more | **WHO FOR** |
| 13 | Software teams for Pakistan, USA, UK, UAE, Canada and Australia | **WHO FOR** |
| 14 | POS, apps, Shopify and portal project profiles | **PROOF** |
| 15 | WordbitX is trusted to deliver excellence worldwide (sphere) | **PROOF** |
| 16 | What clients write about WordbitX | **PROOF** |
| 17 | Guides on software, websites, apps and project cost | **DEPTH** |
| 18 | Questions we are asked before every project | **OBJECTIONS** |
| 19 | Let's build something your business can grow on | **ACT** |

Now read only the theme column, top to bottom:

```
HOOK · WHY · WHY · WHAT · PROOF · WHO · WHAT · WHAT · WHY · HOW · HOW · WHO · WHO · PROOF · PROOF · PROOF · DEPTH · OBJ · ACT
```

**That is the problem.** The four main themes are scattered instead of grouped:

- **WHAT WE DO** → positions **4, 7, 8**
- **PROOF** → positions **5, 14, 15, 16**
- **WHO IT'S FOR** → positions **6, 12, 13**
- **WHY US** → positions **2, 3, 9**

A visitor scrolling this page has to mentally change subject **nine times**. The content is good; the sequencing makes it feel disorganised. This is exactly what you sensed.

### Three specific proofs that the order is wrong, not just my opinion

**a) The portfolio section's own copy gives it away.**
Section 14 reads: *"Scope write-ups of software, mobile and e-commerce work — **separate from the live demo sites above**."*
That sentence was written assuming the demos were just above it. They are **nine sections away**. The copy already knows where it is supposed to be.

**b) Solutions and Industries are the same subject, six sections apart.**
Solutions (6) lists: Real Estate · Healthcare · Education · E-Commerce · Jewellery · Business Management.
Industries (12) lists: Retail · **Real Estate** · **Healthcare** · Pharmacy · **Education** · Logistics · **E-Commerce** · Hospitality · Finance…
**Four direct overlaps.** The visitor reads about real-estate platforms, scrolls through five unrelated sections, then reads about real estate again.

**c) The two trust sections are seven sections apart and say the same thing.**
Both open "WordbitX is trusted to deliver…", both are dark, both rotate, both carry the same honesty line, both show the same six markets.

### On the CTA band specifically — you asked whether it is necessary

**Yes. Keep it. This is one of the most valuable blocks on the page.**

Reasoning:
- The page is **3,629 words / ~16 minutes**. Someone who reaches the bottom has read a lot and has high intent. If the page simply ends in a footer, that intent evaporates into a list of links.
- The header CTA has scrolled away by then. Without this block there is **no ask** at the moment of maximum interest.
- It gives three contact routes (form, WhatsApp, phone) for people with different comfort levels.
- It is already used consistently on **21 pages** — removing it from the home page only would make the home page the odd one out.

The only thing I would consider is making its headline more specific to the home page, but the block itself stays.

## 3. Recommended order

Seven clear acts. Each theme appears **once**.

| | Section | Act |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** — h1, two CTAs, team photo, tech marquee | **HOOK**<br>Who we are, in 5 seconds |
| 2 | **Assurance strip** — own everything · same-day reply · written scope · Pakistan base | **PROMISE**<br>Four concrete commitments |
| 3 | **Services** — "Software, stores, SEO and marketing" (8 cards) | **WHAT WE DO** |
| 4 | **Service ring** — 16 clickable icons *(orbit, moved up, heading removed)* | ↳ *and here is the full map* |
| 5 | **Demos** — "See our work live" | **PROOF**<br>Three kinds of evidence,<br>back to back |
| 6 | **Portfolio** — "Project profiles" *(moved up from 14)* | ↳ |
| 7 | **Reviews** — "What clients write" *(moved up from 16)* | ↳ |
| 8 | **Industries + Solutions** *(merged)* — who we already understand | **WHO IT'S FOR** |
| 9 | **Markets + sphere** *(merged)* — six countries, with the globe as its artwork | ↳ |
| 10 | **Process** — 7 stages, discovery to launch | **HOW WE WORK** |
| 11 | **Technologies** — the stack | ↳ |
| 12 | **Why WordbitX** *(pillars + why-points + the 4 team photos, merged)* | **WHY US**<br>Earned here, not claimed upfront |
| 13 | **Insights** — 3 latest guides | **DEPTH** |
| 14 | **FAQ** — 9 questions | **OBJECTIONS** |
| 15 | **"Let's build something your business can grow on"** | **ACT** |

Theme sequence becomes:

```
HOOK · PROMISE · WHAT · WHAT · PROOF · PROOF · PROOF · WHO · WHO · HOW · HOW · WHY · DEPTH · OBJ · ACT
```

No theme is interrupted. The visitor changes subject **six times instead of nine**, and every change is a step forward in the argument.

## 4. What this costs and what it achieves

Measured from the production build before and after, not estimated.

| | Before | Predicted | **Actual** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `<h2>` headings in page | 17 | — | **12** |
| Content blocks | 19 | 15 | **15** |
| Sections cut outright | — | 1 | 1 ("Three areas…") |
| Sections merged | — | 4 pairs | 4 pairs |
| Sections moved | — | 4 | 4 |
| Markup | 445 KB | ~370 KB | **430 KB** |
| `<img>` tags | 43 | — | **40** |
| Links | 215 | ~180 | **214** |
| Words | 3,629 | ~3,150 | **3,697** |

Two predictions were wrong and it is worth recording why:

- **Links barely moved (215 → 214).** The 15-link hero strip was *not* part of the
  reorder plan, and merging sections preserved every card, so almost no links were
  lost. Removing that strip is still outstanding.
- **Word count went up, not down (3,629 → 3,697).** Cutting "Three areas…" removed
  ~250 words, but the merged sections needed new connecting copy (the ring intro,
  the markets paragraph, six rewritten why-cards), which more than replaced it.

**This change was about order, not weight.** The weight work is the separate list in
`HOME-PAGE-AUDIT.md`, where the 2.1 MB `simple-icons` bug dwarfs everything here.

**Nothing is de-indexed.** No URL is deleted, no slug changes, no redirect, no `noindex`. All 60 service pages stay in the sitemap regardless of home-page layout. The only real content removal is the "Three areas" block (~250 words), and those three services each have a full dedicated page already.

### The argument the page will make, in order

> This is who we are → here is what we promise → here is what we build → here is the whole catalogue → **here is proof you can click, proof you can read, and proof from clients** → this is the kind of business we already understand → these are the countries we serve → this is how we run a project → this is the stack → *now* here is why us → here is more depth → here are your likely questions → let's talk.

Right now that argument is told out of order, with the proof split across four places and "why us" asserted twice before the visitor knows what you sell.

## 5. Suggested sequence of work

This is a layout change, so it is low-risk but worth doing in stages so each step is separately revertible.

| Step | Change | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Move Portfolio up next to Demos; move Reviews up after it | None — pure reorder |
| 2 | Move the orbit up under the services grid, drop its trust heading | None |
| 3 | Merge sphere into the Markets section | None — keeps the section you asked for, kills the duplicate |
| 4 | Move Industries next to Solutions and merge the headings | None |
| 5 | Move the team photos + pillars down into "Why WordbitX" | None |
| 6 | Cut "Three areas where we deliver the most value" | Low — only real content removal |

Steps 1–5 move existing markup around and remove nothing. Step 6 is the only one that deletes copy, so it goes last and can be reverted on its own.
